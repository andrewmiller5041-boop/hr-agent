Company Equipment Policy

This policy covers laptops, monitors, phones, and other equipment issued directly by Northwind Analytics IT.

Standard Issue

All employees receive a company-managed laptop appropriate to their role (standard business laptop, or a higher-spec developer/data laptop for engineering and data roles). Employees may request one external monitor for home or office use through the IT ticketing system.

Company Phones

Company phones are issued only to roles with a documented on-call or client-facing communication need, subject to manager approval. Other employees may use the company mobile device management (MDM) profile on a personal phone for email and calendar access, governed by the Data Security Policy.

Care and Loss

Employees are expected to take reasonable care of issued equipment. If a device is lost, stolen, or damaged, report it to IT and Security within 24 hours (see the Data Security Policy for the 1-hour incident reporting window that applies if the device may have contained accessible customer data). First-time accidental loss or damage is typically covered by the Company; repeated loss (2 or more incidents in 12 months) may result in the employee being charged a replacement deductible of up to $200.

Remote and International Shipping

IT ships standard equipment to remote employees' home address on file. International shipment of replacement equipment requires pre-approval from IT due to customs and cost considerations; see the Remote Work Policy for the broader approval process for working from another country.

Returning Equipment

All company equipment must be returned within 10 business days of an employee's last day of employment, using the prepaid return shipping label provided by IT. Unreturned equipment may be deducted from final pay to the extent permitted by applicable state law.

Home Office Furniture

This policy does not cover home office furniture (desks, chairs) or peripherals purchased by the employee — those are addressed by the home office stipend described in the Business Expense & Reimbursement Policy.
