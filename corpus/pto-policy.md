# Paid Time Off (PTO) Policy

Northwind Analytics ("the Company") provides paid time off so employees can rest,
travel, handle personal matters, and observe holidays or religious occasions not
covered by the company holiday schedule.

## Accrual

Full-time employees accrue PTO at the following rates, credited monthly:

- Years 0-2 of tenure: 15 days per year (1.25 days/month)
- Years 3-5 of tenure: 20 days per year (1.67 days/month)
- Years 6+ of tenure: 25 days per year (2.08 days/month)

Part-time employees (working fewer than 30 hours/week) accrue PTO on a pro-rated
basis proportional to hours scheduled. Interns and contractors are not eligible
for accrued PTO; see the Employment Type section of the Benefits Overview.

## Requesting PTO

1. Employees should submit PTO requests through the HR system at least **5
   business days** in advance for requests of 3 or more consecutive days, and at
   least **2 business days** in advance for shorter requests.
2. All PTO requests require **manager approval** before they are considered
   confirmed. An employee should not treat a submitted request as approved until
   they receive explicit confirmation.
3. Managers should respond to PTO requests within 2 business days. If a request
   is not approved or denied within that window, the employee should follow up
   directly with their manager and may loop in HR if there is no response after
   5 business days.
4. Blackout periods (see below) may require additional lead time or may not be
   approvable at all during high-priority business windows.

## Blackout Periods

Certain teams (e.g., Finance during quarter-end close, Customer Success during
major product launches) may declare blackout periods during which PTO requests
are discouraged or require VP-level approval. Blackout periods are communicated
by team leads at least 30 days in advance.

## Unused PTO and Carryover

Employees may carry over up to **5 unused PTO days** into the next calendar
year. Any accrued PTO beyond that cap is forfeited on December 31st unless
local law requires payout or carryover of a greater amount, in which case local
law controls.

Upon termination of employment, unused, accrued PTO up to the annual cap is paid
out at the employee's final base rate, in accordance with state law where
applicable.

## Sick Time vs. PTO

Northwind Analytics does not maintain a separate sick-time bucket in most U.S.
states; PTO may be used for illness, medical appointments, or caregiving. In
states that legally require a separate paid sick leave bank, employees accrue
sick time separately according to that state's requirements; contact HR for
your state's specific accrual rate.
