# Benefits Overview

This document summarizes core benefits available to Northwind Analytics
employees. Specific plan documents (medical, dental, vision, 401k) are
available separately through the benefits portal; this overview covers
eligibility basics for common employee questions.

## Eligibility by Employment Type

- **Full-time employees** (30+ hours/week): eligible for medical, dental,
  vision, life insurance, and 401(k) with company match, starting the **first
  of the month following 30 days of employment**.
- **Part-time employees** (20-29 hours/week): eligible for 401(k) participation
  (no company match) and a limited voluntary benefits menu (e.g., dental,
  vision buy-up), but not eligible for company-subsidized medical coverage.
- **Part-time employees under 20 hours/week, interns, and contractors**: not
  eligible for the benefits programs described in this document.

## Health Insurance

Full-time employees choose from two medical plan tiers (Standard and Premium)
during open enrollment or within 30 days of a qualifying life event (marriage,
birth/adoption, loss of other coverage). The Company subsidizes 80% of the
employee-only premium and 60% of dependent premiums for the Standard plan.

## 401(k) Retirement Plan

The Company matches 100% of employee 401(k) contributions up to 4% of base
salary for full-time employees, with immediate vesting. Part-time employees
may contribute but do not receive a match.

## Life and Disability Insurance

Full-time employees receive basic life insurance (1x annual salary) and
short-term disability coverage at no cost. Supplemental life insurance and
long-term disability are available for voluntary purchase.

## Parental and Family Leave Benefits

See the Leave of Absence Policy for paid parental leave duration and
eligibility; this document covers only the medical/insurance-side benefits
described above.

## Changing Your Elections

Outside of open enrollment, benefits elections can only be changed within 30
days of a qualifying life event. Contact HR to initiate a life-event change;
retroactive changes beyond the 30-day window are not permitted under IRS
Section 125 rules governing pre-tax benefit elections.
