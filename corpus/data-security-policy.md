# Data Security & Acceptable Use Policy

All Northwind Analytics employees are responsible for protecting company and
customer data, wherever they work.

## Device Requirements

- Only company-managed laptops and phones may be used to access internal
  systems containing customer data (CRM, data warehouse, ticketing system).
- All company devices must have disk encryption, automatic screen lock (max 5
  minutes idle), and up-to-date endpoint security software enabled at all
  times. IT pushes these settings automatically; employees must not disable
  them.
- Personal devices may be used for email and calendar only, via the approved
  mobile management profile, and never for customer data exports.

## Network Requirements

- When working outside the office, employees must connect through the company
  VPN before accessing internal systems.
- Public Wi-Fi (airports, cafes, hotels) must never be used to handle
  confidential data without an active VPN connection.
- Employees traveling internationally should contact IT Security in advance
  in countries with known network-monitoring or VPN-restriction concerns.

## Data Handling

- Customer data must never be exported to personal cloud storage, personal
  email, or unmanaged USB drives.
- Confidential documents (contracts, employee records, financial data) must
  be shared only via approved company file-sharing tools with access limited
  to those who need it.
- Printed materials containing confidential data must be shredded, not placed
  in general recycling or trash.

## Incident Reporting

Any suspected data security incident, including a lost device, phishing
email interaction, or unauthorized data access, must be reported to
security@northwindanalytics.example within **1 hour of discovery**. Prompt
reporting significantly reduces potential impact and is not subject to
disciplinary action on its own; failure to report a known incident is treated
as a policy violation.

## Related Policies

See the Remote Work Policy for how these requirements apply while working
from another state or country, and the Workplace Conduct Policy for
disciplinary procedures related to policy violations.
