# Leave of Absence Policy

This policy covers leave beyond standard PTO, including parental, medical, and
bereavement leave, for eligible Northwind Analytics employees.

## Paid Parental Leave

Full-time employees who have completed at least 90 days of employment are
eligible for **12 weeks of paid parental leave** at 100% of base salary,
following the birth, adoption, or foster placement of a child. Leave must be
taken within 12 months of the qualifying event and can be split into two
blocks with manager and HR approval.

## Medical Leave

Employees with a serious health condition may take medical leave, which runs
concurrently with any federal or state-mandated leave (e.g., FMLA where
applicable). The first 2 weeks of qualifying medical leave are paid at 100%
using available PTO and/or short-term disability benefits; leave beyond that
follows the applicable short-term disability plan's payment schedule.
Employees should provide medical certification within 15 days of the leave
request where feasible.

## Bereavement Leave

Employees receive **5 paid bereavement days** for the death of an immediate
family member (spouse, child, parent, sibling) and **2 paid days** for an
extended family member. Additional unpaid leave may be requested through HR.

## Military Leave

Employees called to active duty or required to attend military training
receive leave in accordance with applicable federal and state law (e.g.,
USERRA), with reemployment rights preserved.

## Requesting a Leave of Absence

1. Notify your manager and HR as soon as the need for leave is known (30 days
   advance notice preferred for foreseeable leave such as parental leave).
2. HR will confirm eligibility, leave type, and pay treatment in writing.
3. Employees on approved leave continue to accrue tenure for PTO accrual-rate
   purposes but do not accrue new PTO days during unpaid portions of leave.
