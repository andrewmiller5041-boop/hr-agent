# New Hire Onboarding Checklist

This checklist outlines standard onboarding steps for new Northwind Analytics
employees during their first two weeks.

## Before Day One

- HR sends offer letter, background check consent, and I-9/tax forms.
- IT provisions company laptop, email account, and shipping to home address
  (remote hires) or desk setup (office-based hires).
- Manager assigns an onboarding buddy from the new hire's team.

## Day One

- New hire completes I-9 verification (in person or via authorized remote
  verification agent) and submits remaining tax/payroll forms.
- IT walks through device setup, VPN configuration, and required security
  training enrollment (see Data Security Policy).
- HR orientation covers benefits enrollment window, PTO accrual start date,
  and the employee handbook.

## First Week

- Complete mandatory compliance training: data security, workplace conduct,
  and anti-harassment training (all required within the first 14 days).
- Meet with manager to set 30/60/90-day goals.
- Enroll in benefits within the 30-day window described in the Benefits
  Overview, if not completed on Day One.

## First 30 Days

- Confirm equipment received and functioning; report any issues per the
  Equipment Policy.
- Shadow onboarding buddy on at least 2 team workflows or meetings.
- Manager check-in at 30 days to review onboarding progress.

## Remote New Hires

Remote new hires follow the same checklist above. IT ships equipment at least
5 business days before the start date where possible. Remote hires working
from a state different from Northwind Analytics's registered payroll states
should flag this to HR immediately during onboarding, since it may require
new state payroll registration — see the Remote Work Policy for the general
process governing location changes after the initial hire.
