# Company Holiday Schedule

Northwind Analytics observes the following paid holidays each year for all
U.S.-based full-time and part-time employees. Employees based outside the
United States follow the holiday schedule for their local Northwind Analytics
entity, published separately by regional HR.

## Standard U.S. Holidays

1. New Year's Day
2. Memorial Day
3. Juneteenth
4. Independence Day
5. Labor Day
6. Thanksgiving Day
7. Day after Thanksgiving
8. Christmas Eve (half day)
9. Christmas Day
10. New Year's Eve (half day)

## Floating Holidays

In addition to the standard holidays above, every employee receives **2
floating holidays** per calendar year to use for religious, cultural, or
personal observances not on the standard schedule. Floating holidays:

- Must be requested through the same system as PTO requests, at least 2
  business days in advance.
- Do not require the manager pre-approval lead time that longer PTO requests
  require, but still require manager acknowledgment.
- Expire at the end of the calendar year and are **not eligible for carryover**
  (unlike standard PTO, which allows limited carryover per the PTO Policy).

## Holidays Falling on Weekends

If a standard holiday falls on a Saturday, it is observed on the preceding
Friday. If it falls on a Sunday, it is observed on the following Monday.

## Office Closures

The corporate office is fully closed on all standard holidays listed above.
Employees are not expected to be reachable on these days. Client-facing teams
with on-call rotations should refer to their team's specific on-call policy,
which is separate from this holiday schedule.
