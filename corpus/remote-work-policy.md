# Remote Work Policy

Northwind Analytics supports flexible and remote work arrangements while
ensuring compliance with tax law, data security requirements, and business
needs.

## Standard Remote Work

Employees whose roles are designated "remote-eligible" by their manager and HR
may work from home or another approved location within their **home state (or
home country, for non-U.S. employees)** without additional approval, subject to
normal performance expectations.

## Temporary Work From Another State or Country

Working temporarily from a state or country other than an employee's registered
home location requires **advance approval** because it can create new tax
withholding obligations, payroll registration requirements, and, for
international travel, immigration or right-to-work considerations for the
Company.

- **Up to 10 consecutive business days** in another U.S. state: requires
  manager approval only. Notify HR via the HR system so payroll can track
  short-term multi-state work.
- **11-30 consecutive business days** in another U.S. state, or **any**
  international remote work up to 30 days: requires manager approval **and**
  sign-off from HR/Payroll, submitted at least **10 business days** in advance,
  due to potential state tax withholding or treaty implications.
- **More than 30 days** in another state or country: requires a formal review
  by HR, Legal, and Payroll. Extended arrangements of this length may require a
  written remote work agreement, and in some cases may not be approvable due to
  entity registration or tax nexus issues in the destination jurisdiction.

Employees should submit remote work location change requests at least 10
business days before travel for any request exceeding 10 business days, to
allow time for the payroll/tax review referenced above.

## Data Security Requirements While Remote

All remote work, regardless of location, must comply with the Data Security
Policy, including:

- Use of company-managed devices or an approved VPN for accessing internal
  systems.
- No use of public, unsecured Wi-Fi for handling confidential company or
  customer data without an active VPN connection.
- Physical safeguarding of devices and printed materials containing company
  data while traveling.

## Equipment While Remote

Employees working remotely, including temporarily from another state or
country, remain responsible for the care of company-issued equipment per the
Equipment Policy. The Company does not ship replacement equipment
internationally except in cases pre-approved by IT.

## Approval Workflow Summary

1. Employee submits a remote work location request with dates and location.
2. Manager reviews for business/coverage impact and approves or denies.
3. For requests over 10 business days (domestic) or any international request,
   HR/Payroll reviews for tax and compliance impact.
4. Employee receives written confirmation before traveling with tax/compliance
   sign-off status.

## Related Policies

See the Expense Policy for reimbursement of any equipment or workspace costs
associated with remote work, and the Data Security Policy for device and
