Workplace Conduct & HR Case Policy

Northwind Analytics is committed to a respectful, safe, and inclusive workplace. This policy describes expected conduct and how concerns are handled.

Expected Conduct

Employees are expected to treat colleagues, customers, and partners with respect; to avoid discrimination or harassment based on any protected characteristic; and to comply with all other Company policies, including the Data Security Policy and Expense Policy.

Reporting a Concern

Employees who experience or witness harassment, discrimination, retaliation, or other workplace misconduct should report it to their manager, HR, or through the anonymous ethics hotline. Reports can be made verbally or in writing, and employees are encouraged to report as soon as possible after an incident, though delayed reporting does not prevent an investigation.

Triage and Escalation

HR reviews every report within 2 business days to determine severity and next steps:

  • Routine policy questions (e.g., "what counts as a conflict of interest") can typically be answered directly by HR or through documented policy guidance.
  • Interpersonal conflicts without safety concerns are typically routed to a manager or HR business partner for informal resolution, with HR oversight.
  • Serious allegations (harassment, discrimination, safety threats, retaliation) are escalated immediately to HR Employee Relations and, where warranted, Legal. These cases are never resolved through self-service or automated guidance alone — a human HR investigator must be assigned.

Confidentiality

Reports are handled as confidentially as possible, shared only with those who need to know to investigate and resolve the matter. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is strictly prohibited and is itself grounds for disciplinary action.

Disciplinary Process

Confirmed policy violations may result in coaching, a formal written warning, suspension, or termination, depending on severity and history, consistent with applicable law and any collective bargaining agreements.

Note on Automated Tools

Any HR assistant or automated tool used by employees to ask workplace questions must escalate sensitive personal complaints to a human HR representative rather than attempting to resolve them automatically. A mock case record may be created to route the concern, but it does not replace a human investigator being assigned.
