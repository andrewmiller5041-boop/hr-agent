# Business Expense & Reimbursement Policy

This policy covers reimbursable business expenses for all Northwind Analytics
employees. All expenses must be reasonable, business-related, and submitted
with a receipt for any amount over $25.

## Home Office Equipment

Employees who are remote-eligible and work from home at least 3 days per week
are eligible for a **one-time home office stipend of up to $500** to cover
items such as:

- An ergonomic chair
- A monitor, keyboard, or mouse
- A desk or sit-stand desk converter
- Basic office supplies

This stipend may be used once every 2 years. Purchases must be submitted with
a receipt within 60 days of purchase. Laptops and primary work computers are
**not** covered under this stipend — see the Equipment Policy for
company-issued hardware.

Employees who are only occasionally remote (fewer than 3 days/week) are not
eligible for the home office stipend but may request small ergonomic
accommodations through HR on a case-by-case basis, especially if supported by
an ergonomic assessment.

## Travel Expenses

Reasonable travel expenses for approved business travel are reimbursable,
including:

- Coach/economy airfare
- Standard hotel accommodations (up to $250/night in major metro areas, $180/
  night elsewhere, unless a conference rate is higher)
- Ground transportation (rideshare, taxi, standard rental car)
- Meals while traveling, up to $75/day, itemized

Business class airfare is reimbursable only for flights longer than 6 hours
with VP-level pre-approval. First class is not reimbursable under any
circumstance.

## Non-Reimbursable Expenses

The following are not reimbursable: personal entertainment, alcohol at
non-client meals, traffic or parking fines, spa or minibar charges, upgrades
without pre-approval, and expenses lacking an itemized receipt over $25.

## Submission and Approval

Expenses must be submitted within 30 days of being incurred through the
expense system, with manager approval required for all submissions and
additional Finance approval required for any single expense over $1,000.

## Related Policies

Equipment such as laptops, monitors issued directly by IT, and phones are
governed by the Equipment Policy, not this expense policy. Expenses incurred
while working remotely from another state or country are still subject to
standard limits above, but see the Remote Work Policy for any additional
approvals required for the underlying travel/location itself.
